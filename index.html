How separate crises become a polycrisis

Polycrisis describes a condition in which separate crises feed into one another across systems. A heat event can raise electricity demand while smoke increases pressure on healthcare. Displacement can reach a housing system that already has little room. The combined condition depends on the structure that connects these pressures.

This diagram is a momentary snapshot. A complete account would need to represent the world system, changing institutions, planetary dynamics, local differences, and countless feedbacks at once. The map instead makes a bounded set of Canadian relationships available for inspection. Every pathway remains conditional.
