How separate crises become a polycrisis

Several crises can occur during the same period without forming a polycrisis. The condition changes when one crisis alters another system's existing stress or its ability to recover. Wildfire displacement may enter a housing market that already has little room. During another event, heat may raise electricity demand while smoke increases demand for healthcare.

A linear cascade can occur within this larger condition. The diagram treats it as one possible pathway. Pressure may arrive from a different system, and the effects may return to conditions that existed before the initial event.
